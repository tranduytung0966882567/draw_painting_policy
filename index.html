Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Draw & Painting handles information when you use our application.

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Draw & Painting ("we", "our", or "the app") is a creative drawing and painting application that allows users to create artwork, use AR drawing features, convert photos into black-and-white sketches, and manage their own artwork.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the application may access certain information required to provide its functionality.

Photos and Images

If you choose to import a photo or image, the app may request access to your device's photo library or media files.

Photos are used only to provide features such as photo editing, sketch conversion, drawing, and artwork creation.

Camera

The AR Draw feature may require access to your device camera. Camera access is used to display the real-world environment and enable AR drawing functionality.

We do not use the camera to identify you personally.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the application may be used to:

  • Provide drawing and painting features
  • Provide AR drawing functionality
  • Convert photos into sketches or black-and-white artwork
  • Save and display your artwork
  • Improve application performance and stability
  • Display advertisements where applicable

4. Artwork and User Content

Artwork created or imported by you is considered your content.

The application may store artwork locally on your device so that you can view and manage your creations.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise in the application, we do not claim ownership of your artwork.

5. Advertising

The application may use third-party advertising services to display advertisements.

These advertising providers may collect certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and interaction with advertisements, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We may use Google AdMob to provide advertisements.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

6. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how users interact with the application and to improve the application experience.

Analytics information may include app usage, device information, crash information, and general interaction data.

7. App Permissions

The application may request the following permissions:

📷
Camera

Used for AR drawing and camera-based features.

🖼️
Photos / Media

Used to select images for drawing, editing, and sketch conversion.

💾
Storage

Used to save and access artwork on supported devices.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services, including advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other services necessary to operate and improve the application.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion of applicable information.
